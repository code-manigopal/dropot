Important — Read Before Continuing

This platform connects to your Google Drive to contribute storage to a shared group pool.

We strongly recommend using a dedicated Google account for privacy and safety.

Even if you connect with your existing Gmail account — your personal files will never be touched. Only files uploaded through this platform to this group will be managed.

Before You Join

  • ✅ Your Google Drive will be connected to this group as shared storage
  • ✅ Only files uploaded through this platform to the group are affected — your existing Google Drive content is never touched
  • ✅ All group files are permanently deleted when the admin dismantles the group
  • ✅ You will be notified before the group dismantles — flag Done only when you've downloaded what you need
  • ✅ Use a dedicated Google account for group sharing — not your personal one
  • ✅ Only share content you own or have the rights to share
  • ✅ No illegal, harmful, or explicit content — violations are the uploader's sole responsibility
  • ✅ This platform is a storage bridge — it does not host, own, or moderate your content
